Apexline is ahead of every audited platform on **pricing from vendor cost up with
a margin floor**, and on **qualifying a prospect against what can actually be
delivered**. It is behind on everything after the work starts — effort, cash,
retention and proof — and that is where a BPO desk actually keeps its money.

No audited platform closes the loop from pricing a deal to proving the margin
landed in cash. That gap is the product.
